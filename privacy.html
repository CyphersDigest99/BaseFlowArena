Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RhymeNexus ("the app"), available at rhymenex.us, handles your information.

1. No Personal Data Collected

RhymeNexus does not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information. We do not have user accounts, email addresses, or any registration system.

2. Local Storage

The app saves your preferences (word list selection, syllable filters, favorites, blacklist, BPM settings) in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

3. Discord Activity Sessions

When used as a Discord Activity, a temporary random session identifier is created and stored in your browser's session storage. This identifier is used only to maintain your connection to the real-time session relay (PartyKit) for the duration of the voice channel session. It is not linked to your Discord account, not logged, and discarded when the session ends.

No message content, voice data, or Discord user information is collected or retained by RhymeNexus.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Discord — When used as a Discord Activity, your use is also subject to Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — The app is hosted on Vercel, which may log standard web server data (IP addresses, request paths) per their own Privacy Policy.
  • PartyKit / Cloudflare — Real-time session relay for multiplayer. Session messages are transient and not stored. Subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

5. Microphone Access

The voice recognition feature requests access to your microphone via your browser's Web Speech API. Audio is processed entirely on your device by your browser — it is not sent to or stored by RhymeNexus.

6. Cookies

RhymeNexus does not use cookies.

7. Children's Privacy

RhymeNexus is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions? Reach out via the RhymeNexus Discord or through the project's GitHub page.
